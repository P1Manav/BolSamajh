Ministry of Electronics and IT Circular: DPDP Guidelines 2026

Circular Reference: MeitY/DPDP/2026/04

Section 1: Data Localization and Cross-Border Transfers

Data fiduciaries handling personal voice records, health transcripts, and financial records of Indian data principals must ensure all critical personal data is stored within servers physically located in the territory of India.

Cross-border transfer of non-critical data is permitted only to jurisdictions explicitly notified on the government whitelist.

Section 2: Consent and Multilingual Transparency

Notice for consent must be made available in English and all 22 languages specified in the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution, including Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, and Bengali.

Withdrawal of consent must be as effortless as granting consent.

Section 3: Data Breach Penalties

Failure to implement reasonable security safeguards resulting in a significant personal data breach attracts financial penalties of up to Rs 250 Crore per instance.
